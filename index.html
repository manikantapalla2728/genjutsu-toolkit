Complete Creation Workflow

Download the Reference Video

Save the 20-second reference MP4 file provided above directly to your phone or computer.

2

Prepare 3 Character Photos

Select clear, front-facing portrait photos with good lighting. You can use photos of yourself, friends, fictional characters, or celebrities.

3

Open Higgsfield AI & Select GENJUTSU

Navigate to higgsfield.ai, log in, and choose the GENJUTSU motion transfer tool.

4

Upload Video & Character Images

Upload the reference video into the video container. Next, upload your three portrait images into slots 1 (Middle), 2 (Right), and 3 (Left).

5

Paste Prompt & Generate

Click the Copy Prompt button above, paste it into the prompt box, and hit Generate! In 1–2 minutes, your video will be ready for export.
